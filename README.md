# GreenThreads Denver Operations Assistant

## About This Project

This portfolio documents my work as an Operations analyst for the GreenThreads Denver store launch project in AI.205: AI Integration in Business I.

Throughout the quarter, I used AI to analyze operational workflows, evaluate supplier reliability, validate shipment data, identify cross-functional inconsistencies, and build an evidence-based Operations assistant.

The purpose of the assistant is not to make business decisions automatically. It helps Operations identify risks, validate calculations, find missing information, and provide evidence-based recommendations for human managers.

## Project Development

### HW1 – Operations Functional Brief
Mapped the GreenThreads Operations workflow and identified high-value AI opportunities, including shipment-risk detection, supplier reliability scoring, and inventory planning.

### HW2 – Supplier Reliability and Document Intelligence
Analyzed supplier documents, contracts, shipment history, and OPS-04 requirements. Identified historical delivery risk and gaps in shipment visibility.

### HW3 – Denver Launch Readiness
Analyzed 96 historical shipment records and quantified supplier reliability and launch exposure. Recommended milestone-gated exception control for high-risk launch shipments.

### HW4 – Custom Operations Assistant
Built and tested the GreenThreads Denver Operations Assistant. Testing identified a calculation-consistency error, which led to a new verification rule and a successful retest.

### HW5 – Executive Brief
Connected the entire quarter into one executive recommendation and identified a major cross-functional control gap: Active Shorts had no purchase order even though Finance showed a pending Mekong transaction.

## Current Operations Finding

As of September 8, 2026:

- Denver opens November 1, 2026.
- Launch inventory is required in store by October 19.
- Active Shorts: 1,200 units, no purchase order placed.
- EcoFleece Hoodie: no purchase order placed, with a September 12 order-by date.
- Bamboo Joggers and Classic Tee have confirmed purchase orders.

The project demonstrates how AI can help make operational risks and data inconsistencies visible earlier while keeping final decisions with human managers.

## AI and Human Oversight

AI was used for document analysis, calculation validation, supplier comparison, data-quality checking, and executive summarization.

The assistant remains advisory. Operations, Procurement, Finance, Legal, and GreenThreads management remain responsible for final decisions.
